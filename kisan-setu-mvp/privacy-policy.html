Privacy Policy

Kisan Setu (किसान सेतु) — Last updated: March 9, 2026

1. Introduction

Kisan Setu ("we", "our", "us") is a WhatsApp-based agricultural advisory service built for the AI for Bharat Hackathon 2026 powered by AWS. This policy explains how we collect, use, and protect information when you interact with our service via WhatsApp.

2. Information We Collect

When you use Kisan Setu through WhatsApp, we may collect:

  • Your WhatsApp phone number (provided by the WhatsApp Business API)
  • Messages you send to the service (text, voice notes, and images of crop documents)
  • Agricultural data you share (crop details, land information, location for weather/satellite queries)

3. How We Use Your Information

We use the collected information solely to:

  • Provide personalized agricultural advisory responses
  • Process crop document images for data extraction
  • Generate credit scores and financial recommendations
  • Retrieve relevant satellite imagery and weather data for your region
  • Improve the quality and accuracy of our service

4. Data Storage and Security

Your data is stored securely using AWS services (DynamoDB, S3) in the Asia Pacific (Mumbai) region. We implement encryption at rest and in transit. Conversation data is retained for service improvement and may be deleted upon request.

5. Third-Party Services

We use the following third-party services to operate:

  • Meta WhatsApp Business API — for messaging
  • Amazon Web Services (AWS) — for cloud infrastructure, AI/ML processing (Amazon Bedrock), and data storage

We do not sell, rent, or share your personal information with any other third parties.

6. Your Rights

You have the right to:

  • Request access to the data we hold about you
  • Request deletion of your data
  • Stop using the service at any time by simply not messaging the WhatsApp number

7. Data Retention

We retain conversation data and user profiles for the duration of the service. If you wish to have your data removed, please contact us and we will delete your records within 30 days.

8. Children's Privacy

Our service is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect information from children.

9. Changes to This Policy

We may update this privacy policy from time to time. Any changes will be reflected on this page with an updated date.

10. Contact Us

If you have questions about this privacy policy or your data, please reach out via the Kisan Setu WhatsApp service or contact the development team.
